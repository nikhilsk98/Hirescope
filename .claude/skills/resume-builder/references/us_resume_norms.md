# US resume norms (software engineering)

- One page for students and early-career roles (config `page_count`, default 1). US Letter.
- No photo, age, date of birth, marital status, nationality, visa or citizenship status.
- Reverse chronological within Experience; dates `Mon YYYY – Mon YYYY`, `Present` for current roles,
  `Expected Mon YYYY` for degrees in progress.
- Contact: name, city/state, email, phone, LinkedIn (GitHub / portfolio only if real).
- Plain one-column layout an ATS can parse: no tables, text boxes, icons or images; standard section names
  (Technical Skills, Education, Experience, Projects).
- Co-op / internship: education and projects carry more weight (they come before Experience); full-time:
  experience first.
